# Current phase and approvals

New inquiry preparation proceeds directly to one SendApprovedReply combined
review under [email-flow](email-flow.md). Preparing its inert task/form is not a
send. A checked email-review-v4 Consent can execute on run-auto; reply-draft
itself remains no-send. Do not create a new ReviewReplyDraft task first.

Legacy ReviewReplyDraft/ReviewGeneralReply Confirm decisions cannot supply
sending permission. Preserve the old history and use a current combined sending
decision for remaining work. A previously genuine send approval keeps its exact
scope. Accepted/unknown provider actions remain protected through upgrades.

Manual Notes/persona approvals concern CRM facts and never substitute for an
email approval. Preparation never waits for its future Sent/CRM receipt/SMS
outcome. Missing downstream records recover only their own unfinished step.

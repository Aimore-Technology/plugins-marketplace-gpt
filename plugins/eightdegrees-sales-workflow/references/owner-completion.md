# Completion

Normal new email uses [email-flow](email-flow.md) helpers and API acknowledgements,
without a persisted owner plan at every step. Preparation ends at its one review;
email.complete records the successful provider handoff plus affected own writes.
CRM receipt and SMS registration may recover independently without resending.

For manual CRM workflows and explicit legacy/other-owner repair,
execution.prepare/ready and owner-write use execution-api-v1 receipts for their
actual affected roles. Do not add unrelated pages or future phase outcomes.
report-write remains report-only. Confirm final mutations from their original
API responses/IDs. No saved content, seven-page projection or preview-byte audit.
Keep current approvals/stops/windows/counts/attempts before the actual effect.

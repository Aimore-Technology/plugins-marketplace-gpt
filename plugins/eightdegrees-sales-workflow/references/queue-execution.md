# Shared queue

FIRST honor the live scan switch and authorized discovery scope. Complete
current mail/action/control/due/recovery discovery with no runnable demand exits
immediately. Pending unfinished source handoffs remain demand even with unchanged
mail/index/task rows. Preserve every source key, original task and provider attempt.

New inquiry work follows [email-flow](email-flow.md): one source read, one scoped
CRM match/name fallback, cached exact project/lot materials, one plain-text draft,
one combined content-and-send decision, original Gmail acknowledgement and
standing-authorized SMS registration/dispatch when due. Do not insert a manual
communication/Notes review into this path. Project ambiguity or missing required
material opens only its targeted input. CRM recording failure is independent.

Explicit add-communication starts its own manual Notes/persona review. Current
submitted Manual communication data/decisions resume under their original route;
other unrelated Manual sends are not adopted by scans. Keep Mode provenance.
Reports, reminders and confirmed Calendar work retain their narrower scope.

Use the complete saved queue with stops first and current phase estimates.
One non-fitting item or blocked customer cannot hide other fitting work. Respect
original cycle budget/deadline and save all remaining pointers. Do not sleep,
recursively restart scans or create per-email native tasks. Native and manual
run-auto policies remain distinct; real stop/no-send restrictions are binding.

A human submission is read once and persisted against its exact active binding.
Unchanged waits do not rerun owners; one combined Approve can send its frozen
package under current controls. Clearing a consumed checkbox does not erase
its saved decision. Edits/withdrawal invalidate pending approval. New versions
have blank choices. Legacy Confirm alone never becomes send permission.

API acknowledgement/ID confirms own writes; accepted/pending/unknown provider
effects cannot replay after restart or a later projection failure. Save compact
actual result/wait/block/time metrics at the boundary. No per-step saved-plan,
extra admission record, historical sweep or successful-write body comparison.

## After you submit

Notion saves edits automatically. Checking the named submission to-do requests
processing; it does not mean accepted, executed or sent. Each action page shows
whether to wait for a verified native task or run a specific continuation.
An unconfigured/stopped schedule does not process submissions in the background.

The lists read existing tasks, including older unresolved work. An empty list
means no matching saved tasks, not a new mailbox scan or complete channel check.

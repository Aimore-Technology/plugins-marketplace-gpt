owner command, invented continuation modifier or extra SMS approval is allowed.

1. init from a fresh root, or explicitly upgrade existing settings. Check the
   same database IDs and five human issue views plus complete ScheduledWork
   discovery. Default notification permission is enabled without a per-SMS
   question; recorded explicit disable/revocation and unknown/stop history stay.
2. add-communication manually initiates P0-A. run-auto observes its current
   form but leaves unsubmitted/old-page/changed input waiting. Submit matching,
   data and Notes/persona content in Notion, then run-auto resumes without a
   continue command, preserves Mode=Manual and appends one reviewed CRM Note.
3. Send an ordinary inquiry. Fill missing authorized project sources in Notion.
   run-auto follows each newly ready source/draft/review stage in one bounded
   cycle until the next real human boundary. Separately approve email content
   and exact email sending version/window; run-auto sends once and registers
   its downstream notification. reply-draft/report do not send by themselves.
4. When notification timing and actual channel checks pass, the shared queue
   generates/freezes and sends the SMS without a ContentReview/SendApproval form
   or extra chat permission. Verify actual device/recipient/body digest and
   provider status separately from receipt. Ready/future work is read-only;
   only actual missing data/capability/error needs a human issue task.
5. Stop/opt-out, Pause, authority revocation, missing relevant reply coverage,
   unknown result/count and manual+AI two-message cap still block dispatch.
   An expired legacy exception is not silently caught up. An explicitly no-send
   invocation, setup/report/draft command and restricted old job cannot send.
6. Repeat unchanged scans and material events; retain original cycle/count/due,
   source/Notes/draft/provider IDs with no duplication. Test ready successors,
   human waits, future due times, budget exhaustion and partial pagination:
   no recursion/sleep loop, false completeness or replay after saving failure.
7. report shows actual queue/waits/results without execution. Current Today and
   notification results show no individual SMS review or continue prompt. Test
   native repetition/start/stop separately in the actual supported host.

Record version, expected/actual operation and original evidence. Simulated human
submissions are allowed when authorized; simulation and offline checks do not
prove real delivery, scheduler runtime or independent Beta readiness.

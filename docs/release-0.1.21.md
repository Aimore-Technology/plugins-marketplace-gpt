# EightDegrees Sales Workflow 0.1.21

Operators need one place to find their work, understand the exact review scope
and know what happens after submitting. This release adds Aimore/Today with
native linked lists over existing task records. Actions retains original input
pages and history. Original five execution-discovery views are preserved.

- Init/explicit upgrade registers Today and its display views separately. Native
  task-detail layouts may hide technical properties without deleting values.
  Customized layouts stay intact; unavailable capabilities are disclosed.
- Reviews lead with three business lines and revision differences, then retain
  full same-version material/preview before decision controls. Verified applicable
  setup data is reused; only missing/invalid inputs are editable and draft answers
  stay present. Content confirmation and sending approval remain separate.
- Feedback distinguishes saved edits, requests, accepted input, processing,
  pauses, expired windows, completion and unknown outcomes. Without a verified
  native job it shows the original owner continuation or one public no-send scan.
  An existing exact-version/window approval needs no duplicate chat permission.
- Short daily summaries and offline outcome rendering keep email Sent, SMS
  network/delivery, human receipt and stop observations separate, with evidence
  times and original links. Partial coverage never becomes a global zero.
- Verified linked-view mode removes duplicate Actions queue reads/writes.
  local_checks.py runs named existing checks over one original capture bundle;
  notion_mutation.py --output saves large exact checked calls without echoing
  whole page bodies again. Fresh pre-action and saved-result checks remain.

Both editions ship shared rules/helpers/tests; runtime adapters and manifests
remain edition-specific. These offline helpers do not execute connectors, grant
approval, intercept MCP calls or create a scheduler. Existing installations use
their legacy queue until explicit setup/upgrade verifies the new views.

Verification: 276 offline tests per edition, marketplace/reference checks,
deterministic archives and shared-file parity. Live maintenance verified six
native views and preserved schema, IDs, decisions, settings and review payload.
No new customer send, CRM write, Calendar operation or schedule was needed.
Full installed-model workflow, visual/mobile behavior and measured latency
remain follow-up acceptance; no speed or channel-readiness claim is made.

## Next work and comparison method

Prioritize exact final email transport behavior, the communication DataCompletion route and consistent owner preflight/gate execution. The Experience library packages the evidence and known practices; it is not those fixes. Retain the raw observations and do not rerun live sends solely to turn old failed history green.

For the next authorized run, keep the eleven prior-family regression IDs stable and report them separately from new cases. For each, show observed failures, recovery, final unresolved status, whether the original operation was dispatched, and evidence of check execution before its dependency. Count accepted requests separately from pre-dispatch validation refusals, provider acceptance separately from receipt, and publication separately from business acceptance. This makes recurrence visible without hiding failures or inflating the number of newly introduced defects.
